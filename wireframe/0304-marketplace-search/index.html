UX Notes

  • ① Prominent search icon in the header triggers a full-screen overlay. This gives search maximum dedicated space — the entire screen becomes a search experience.
  • ② Recent searches provide quick re-access to past queries. Category grid and trending listings offer zero-typing discovery paths. The overlay is a search command center.
  • ③ Trade-off: search requires an extra tap to access (icon then overlay), but once opened gets full-screen real estate. Best for users who search intentionally vs. casually.

Recommendation: Option 2 (Search Hub) — The dedicated search zone with its own heading, large input, trending tags, and category shortcuts gives search the most prominent dedicated space on screen. Unlike a simple search bar (Option 1) or a sticky bar that competes with scroll space (Option 3), the Search Hub creates a clear "find" zone that is impossible to miss. It bridges search-intent users (who type) and browse-intent users (who tap categories/trends) in one cohesive section, maximizing discoverability without sacrificing the Visual Cards pattern below.
