Summary & recommendation

All five options share the same information but arrange it for very different intents. Ranked against overall UX quality for a buyer-facing listing details page:

OptionApproachBest forScore
1. Refined Stack Existing vertical pattern, improved hierarchy and quick-facts strip Low-risk ship; mobile parity ★★★☆☆
2. Split View ★ Sticky right-rail contact card, scrollable left column Desktop buyers who need price, trust, and CTA always in view ★★★★★
3. Tabbed Hub Hero above the fold, tabs for Overview/Info/Reviews/Location/Seller Listings with deep sub-content (esp. reviews, menus) ★★★★☆
4. Sticky CTA Single column, immersive hero, floating contact bar docked to viewport Mobile-first users; impulse contacts ★★★★☆
5. Storyboard Scroll Scrollytelling with numbered sections and editorial tone Featured/premium listings, high-intent browsing, hero items ★★★☆☆
Recommended: Option 2 — Split View. Sticky right-rail patterns (Airbnb, Zillow, Facebook Marketplace) win for listing details because they decouple browsing from deciding: the buyer explores photos/highlights/hours on the left while the price, seller trust, and Contact Seller button stay anchored on the right. For Nepally's metro-first community marketplace — where every contact flows through in-app messages — a persistent CTA materially reduces drop-off.

Good fallbacks: Option 4 (Sticky CTA) carries the same CTA-always-visible benefit to mobile and is the natural responsive counterpart. Option 3 (Tabbed Hub) is the strongest choice once the listing model grows reviews and menu content. Option 1 stays available as a safe migration baseline.
